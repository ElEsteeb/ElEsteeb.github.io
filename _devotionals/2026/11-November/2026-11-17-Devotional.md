Today, as you walk through life, you may feel overwhelmed, tired, or anxious. This is life here under the sun. The Bible makes it clear that, left to ourselves, we are insufficient, inclined toward evil, and surrounded by a world filled with hostility toward God. It is easy to despair as we consider the darkness around us.

But God...

Paul continues his explanation of the wonderful gospel he proclaims. He begins by describing our inclusion among God's covenant people, not through a circumcision performed by human hands, but through one accomplished by God, putting away not merely a portion of flesh, but the body of flesh. In Christ, we have been made His people. Once we were not a people, but God...

Paul reminds us that we were not merely sick with sin, nor simply stained by some passing encounter with evil. We were dead in our trespasses. Like criminals found dead at the scene of our own crimes, we lay helpless and hopeless, unable to do anything to save ourselves. But God...

And like those criminals, we were already guilty. What defense could we offer? How could the dead stand before the Judge and plead their innocence? Our guilt was undeniable, our condition irreversible by any power of our own. But God...

We stood condemned before a perfect and all-knowing God. The sentence had already been pronounced: the soul who sins shall die. There was no righteousness of our own by which we could escape that judgment. But God...

In our rebellion, we were subject to the hostile powers of this world, the very powers that stand against the righteous rule of God. We had no strength to overcome them, no victory to claim for ourselves. But God...

God has made us His people. God has made us alive together with Christ. God has forgiven our trespasses and canceled the record of debt that stood against us. God has removed our condemnation by nailing that record to the cross of our Lord and Savior. And through His Son, God has disarmed the hostile powers, putting them to open shame in Christ's triumph.

The world lay in darkness, but God...

The Christian testimony is not, "I once was a bad person, but one day I..." It is, "I once was dead, but God..." We rest in the security of His loving action, press forward in His strength, and do not fear the evil day, because Christ has conquered all.

Our circumstances may overwhelm us. Our strength may fail us. The world may rise against us.

**But God...**
